Question 4

Question 5
A car travels first half distance at 40 km/hr and second half at 60 km/hr.
Find average speed.


1.Solution:
speed=distance/time
     =60km/1 hrs
     =60km/hr.

2.Solution:
time=distance/speed
    =150/50
    =3 hours.

3.Solution:
Distance=speed*time
        =5*3
        =15km.

4.Solution:
When two objects move in opposite directions, their speeds are added
=40+60
=100km.

5.Solution:
average speed=2xy/x+y
             =2*40*60/40+60
             =4800/100
             =48km/hrs.
